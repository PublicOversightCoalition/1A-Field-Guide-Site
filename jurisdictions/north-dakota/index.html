    What this status means¶

    No Coalition-qualified state Field Guide framework has been activated. This does not mean no constitutional or federal law applies, and it does not mean research is underway. It means the jurisdiction-specific framework has not yet passed the Coalition qualification gate.

    Facility-research maturity: Not assigned until the Field Guide is Active.

    Qualification path¶

    Qualification is based on operational usability, not page count or popularity. The jurisdiction must map the canonical scenario/encounter families and resolve each material branch as Supported, Researched-Unresolved, Gap, or Not Applicable.

    A material Gap blocks activation. Genuine unresolved law may remain Researched-Unresolved when the research supports that conclusion.

    Read the jurisdiction qualification method and the project-wide Constitution First method.

    Contributing toward qualification¶

    Structured research can help develop this jurisdiction even before activation. Useful contributions include primary authority, binding/persuasive classification, contrary authority, statutory/regulatory text, procedural posture, corrections, and well-supported unresolved results.

    The authenticated Coalition intake is not yet deployed in this public-shell milestone. The contribution/review model is defined, but this page does not imply that a division or research team is currently active.

    Federal and circuit authority¶

    Federal constitutional protection and applicable federal/circuit authority remain part of the analysis regardless of this jurisdiction's qualification status. Federal circuits are an applicable legal-authority layer and are not modeled as child jurisdictions of states.
