# Iowa Army Ammunition Plant: Historical Storage and Regulatory Oversight

**Jurisdiction:** Federal, Iowa  
**Status:** ACTIVE · INITIAL RESEARCH  
**Opened:** November 25, 2026

[Back to Research & Investigations](../../investigations/index.md)

## Why we opened this investigation

The Coalition is examining the history of lead azide storage at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant and the circumstances leading to emergency disposal. Our focus is whether earlier inspections, storage practices, corrective action or regulatory intervention could have prevented the emergency. The merits of the emergency response itself are a separate question.

## Questions under investigation

- What inspections and material condition assessments were performed from 2009 onward, and what did they identify?
- What storage and temperature controls were required, maintained and monitored?
- When did the Army, its contractors and environmental regulators first identify deterioration or recommend disposal?
- What corrective actions were considered or taken, and why?
- What environmental monitoring establishes the actual consequences of the disposal?

## Records we intend to seek

Historical inspection reports, storage and temperature records, contractor warnings, corrective action records, permit history, interagency correspondence, disposal assessments and environmental sampling results.

No records request is represented as submitted at this stage. No conclusion about preventability, legal compliance or contamination has been reached.

## Next steps

Establish a dated source inventory, identify responsible custodians and prepare targeted Army and EPA records requests. Publish supported findings and privacy-reviewed evidence as the investigation develops.
