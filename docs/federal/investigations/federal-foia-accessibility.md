# Federal FOIA Accessibility and Requester Privacy

**Jurisdiction:** Federal, nationwide  
**Status:** ACTIVE · INITIAL RESEARCH  
**Opened:** November 25, 2026

[Back to Research & Investigations](../../investigations/index.md)

## Why we opened this investigation

The Coalition is examining whether federal records-request procedures create unnecessary barriers to obtaining nonpersonal agency records. The inquiry begins with the EPA and ICE/DHS, with DOJ's national FOIA portal included in the comparison. Access for people without a stable mailing address, telephone, identification documents or their own computer is central to this investigation.

## Questions under investigation

- Which submission methods are authorized, and which are actually available?
- Can a person with only an email address request and receive nonpersonal records?
- What identity information, account registration, authentication and security agreements are required?
- What alternatives or accommodations exist for people unable to meet those conditions?
- What administrative reasons and legal authority support the requirements, and what less restrictive alternatives were considered?
- How are requester information and account data handled by agencies and contractors?

## Initial observations and limits

The EPA portal registration and security screens, and the agency listing on FOIA.gov, are initial research leads. We will preserve sanitized copies and compare the observed steps with published rules. We have not established that documentary identity verification is required for ordinary requests, that any particular requirement is unlawful, or that agencies intended to obstruct access.

## Next steps

Document actual intake workflows across agencies, seek written clarification of accommodations and identify rulemaking and administrative records explaining the requirements. No records request is represented as submitted at this stage.

The investigation concerns meaningful public access and requester privacy, not an attempt to bypass lawful security controls.
