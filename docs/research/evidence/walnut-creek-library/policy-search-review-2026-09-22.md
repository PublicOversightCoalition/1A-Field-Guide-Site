## Photography / videography finding

The finding does not establish:

- absence of privacy or conduct rules applicable to particular conduct;

## Current room-source discrepancy

The 2025-revised system Room Use PDF says arrangements for both Oak View and Las Trampas are through City Facility Rentals.

Current live sources now split the channels:

- **Oak View:** City Facility Rentals;
- **Las Trampas:** direct CCCL/online booking.

The current City Oak View page expressly says smaller rooms such as Las Trampas are reserved directly through CCCL and are not facilitated by the City rental office. Current CCCL Rooms/Las Trampas pages agree.

Working treatment:

`CURRENT-LIVE-SOURCES-SUPPORT-CCCL-LAS-TRAMPAS-BOOKING / OLDER-PDF-DISCREPANCY-PRESERVED`

The older PDF remains relevant history and could matter if a formal underlying control agreement is later disputed, but it should not silently override the newer mutually corroborating current-use sources.

## Study-room expression rule

Current Walnut Creek Group Study Room rules expressly prohibit:

- advertising;
- petitions;
- solicitations;
- sales.

This rule belongs to the study-room channel and must not be generalized to the ordinary Library interior, meeting rooms, civic plaza, sidewalks, park, or parking areas.

Its constitutional validity has not been finally adjudicated by this branch sweep. The room remains a purpose-limited channel requiring its own forum/rule analysis.
