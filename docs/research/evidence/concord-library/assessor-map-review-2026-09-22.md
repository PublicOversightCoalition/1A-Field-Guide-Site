## Official-record notation convention

The `OR` abbreviation can now be interpreted with substantially greater confidence because other official Contra Costa County-hosted records expressly pair a full citation with the same shorthand convention.

For example, a County-hosted legal description states that a deed was recorded in **Book 1396 of Official Records, Page 312**, then later refers to the same parcel as `(1396 OR 312)`:

https://www.contracosta.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/73288/Appendix-F---Hazards-Hazardous-Materials-and-Wildfire-Supporting-Info-PDF

Another County-hosted title/legal-description record uses the same pattern, including shorthand such as `(535 OR 213)` after describing records by Book and Page of Official Records:

https://www.contracosta.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/52264/Appendix-M---Phase-I-Environmental-Site-Assessment-PDF

Accordingly, the assessor-sheet notations are now reasonably read as:

- `3356 OR 502` → **Book 3356 of Official Records, Page 502**;
- `636 OR 390` → **Book 636 of Official Records, Page 390**.

This resolves the **citation format**, not the content or legal effect of either instrument. The underlying records still need to be retrieved before the project states what they conveyed, reserved, dedicated, or otherwise established.
