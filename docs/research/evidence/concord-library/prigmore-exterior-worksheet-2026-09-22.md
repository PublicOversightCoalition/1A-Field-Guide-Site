# Concord Library: Exterior / Prigmore Working Worksheet

**Facility:** Concord Library, 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, CA 94519  
**Prepared:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** DOCTRINAL-WORKSHEET / FINAL-AREA-CLASSIFICATION-PENDING

This worksheet applies the property-specific method reflected in *Prigmore v. City of Redding*, 211 Cal.App.4th 1322 (2012), to the currently known Concord facts. It is intentionally preliminary. It does not assign one forum status to the entire Library/Civic Center exterior.

## Method

For each exterior area, keep separate:

1. ownership / parcel;
2. operational control;
3. public ROW or easement status;
4. ordinary public access and use;
5. physical design and relationship to the facility;
6. congregation / passage features;
7. posted restrictions and actual practice;
8. the exact expressive activity at issue;
9. forum classification and applicable restriction standard.

A fact established for one area does not automatically carry into another.

### Unresolved

### Working doctrine posture

If a particular segment is confirmed to be an ordinary municipal sidewalk within the public street ROW, ordinary traditional-public-forum sidewalk doctrine becomes the principal rule. *Prigmore* then supplies useful library-site context but is not necessary to transform an ordinary public sidewalk into a forum.

**Current status:** STRONGLY-SUPPORTED-PUBLIC-STREET-CONTEXT / EXACT-STRIP-MAPPING-PENDING.

## Area 2: Front Salvio parking lot

### Established or strongly supported facts

- CCCL expressly describes a free public front parking lot serving the branch.
- Published inventory: 3 accessible, 3 short-term/30-minute, 15 regular spaces.
- The lot is part of the public-facing approach to the Library.
- Current GIS/assessor evidence resolves the Library parcel and adjacent Civic Center parcel, but the exact parking-lot overlay has not yet been completed with current survey/ROW evidence.

### *Prigmore*-relevant similarities

- government/library setting;
- public parking use;
- physical relationship to library access;
- likely pedestrian movement between parking and entrance.

### Material unknowns

- exact parcel/control of each portion of the lot/driveway;
- whether the lot functions as a pedestrian/congregation area or primarily vehicle storage/circulation;
- benches, newspaper racks, plazas, shade/seating, or other congregation features;
- customary nonparking public use;
- signs or restrictions;
- exact relationship between the lot and public sidewalk/entry path.

### Working doctrine posture

*Prigmore* is a strong comparator but does not create a categorical rule that all public-library parking lots are traditional public forums.

**Current status:** PRIGMORE-COMPARATOR-STRONG / CLASSIFICATION-UNRESOLVED.

## Area 3: Main entrance / forecourt / pedestrian approach

### Established or strongly supported facts

- ordinary patrons use this area to enter and leave the Library during staffed hours;
- City/Civic Center mapping confirms the Library's physical relationship to Salvio, Parkside, parking, and Civic Center circulation;
- the Library parcel is City-owned in current GIS and labeled LIBRARY on the assessor sheet.

### *Prigmore*-relevant similarities

The entry/exit area in *Prigmore* received separate area-specific analysis. For Concord, the entrance/forecourt is therefore a distinct candidate forum area rather than merely an extension of the ordinary interior.

### Material unknowns

- exact physical dimensions/layout;
- whether the approach lies wholly within APN 111-240-006 or partly within ROW/shared Civic Center space;
- benches/seating or congregation features;
- customary waiting, meeting, information-distribution, or community use;
- signs and circulation/security rules;
- whether the area functions principally as passage or also as an intentionally/publicly used gathering space.

### Working doctrine posture

No classification yet. The combination of boundary evidence and physical/site-use facts is outcome-relevant.

**Current status:** DISTINCT-AREA-IDENTIFIED / PRIGMORE-ANALYSIS-PENDING.

## Evidence gaps that actually matter to classification

The remaining boundary work is no longer an abstract title exercise. The highest-value missing facts are the ones that distinguish:

- ordinary municipal sidewalk from parcel-internal walkway;
- Library parcel from shared Civic Center parcel;
- public street ROW from parking/driveway/forecourt;
- Library-controlled parking from Civic Center-controlled/shared parking;
- passage-only space from an area designed or customarily used for congregation/expression.

Current City Engineering/ROW records can resolve much of the first three categories. A later field/site-layout verification can resolve physical design, signs, seating, and customary-use observations.

## Provisional doctrinal order after boundary acquisition

Once current ROW/control evidence is obtained, analyze in this order:

1. **confirmed Salvio/Parkside municipal sidewalks**, ordinary public-sidewalk / traditional-public-forum doctrine;
2. **entrance/forecourt**, area-specific *Prigmore* comparison;
3. **front parking lot**, property-specific *Prigmore* comparison;
4. **rear Civic Center parking/circulation**, separate *Prigmore* / government-campus analysis based on its own control/use record;
5. **internal pedestrian paths**, classify individually based on ownership/control, access, purpose, design, and practice.

No current evidence supports collapsing these into one exterior forum classification.
