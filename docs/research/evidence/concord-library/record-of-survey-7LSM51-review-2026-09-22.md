## Human-reviewed map observations

The rendered survey sheet states that it depicts:

> A portion of the property of the Concord Home Acres Company.

It identifies that company's deed as **Book 454 of Official Records, Page 154**, and states that the property is located in Rancho Monte del Diablo.

The survey further states that the **course of Salvio Street, as extended, was taken as the basis for bearings**. The drawing depicts a 10.60-acre tract and shows:

- the Sacramento Northern Railway along the west side;
- Willow Pass Road along the south side, with directional notes toward Concord and Pittsburg;
- a line labeled as the centerline/course of Salvio Street as extended;
- iron-bar and iron-pipe monument references;
- survey bearings and distances around the depicted tract.

The sheet is dated June 1940 and shows County approval/filing in September 1940.

## What this record supports
