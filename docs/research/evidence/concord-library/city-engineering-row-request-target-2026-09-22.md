## Preferred initial request

Before making a broad request, ask Engineering whether it can identify the controlling current map/plan/document numbers:

> I am researching the present property/right-of-way configuration around Concord Library and the Concord Civic Center. Please identify any current right-of-way map, record of survey, street dedication/acceptance map, engineering plan, parcel/tract map, or other official record showing the present public right-of-way edges of Salvio Street and Parkside Drive adjacent to APNs 111-240-006 and 111-240-014. Please also identify any dedication, easement, right-of-entry, or other recorded document referenced by those plans that affects the sidewalks, driveways, entrance approaches, or parking/circulation areas immediately adjoining those parcels. If no single current map exists, please identify the controlling plan/map/document numbers used by the City to determine those ROW edges.

Please also provide any existing City record identifying which agency or department controls or maintains the sidewalks, driveways, entrance approaches, and public parking/circulation areas immediately adjoining Concord Library at 2900 Salvio Street and the Civic Center parcel at 1950 Parkside Drive. Electronic copies are preferred where available.

## Separate operational-control target

If the boundary record does not answer control allocation, a separate request should seek:

> Any current agreement, lease, license, memorandum of understanding, maintenance agreement, parking agreement, joint-use agreement, or similar operative document between the City of Concord and Contra Costa County / Contra Costa County Library concerning Concord Library at 2900 Salvio Street, including exterior parking, sidewalks/approaches, security, maintenance, or shared Civic Center circulation.

Keeping this second request separate makes it easier to distinguish **property/ROW evidence** from **operational-control evidence**.
