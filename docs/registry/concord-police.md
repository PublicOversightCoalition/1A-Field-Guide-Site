## Recording posture

Recording public-facing police activity from a lawful, noninterfering vantage retains strong activity-specific support from CPD Policy 423, Penal Code §148(g), Fordyce, Askins, Project Veritas, and the local Wilson comparator. The 2026 Second Circuit Massimino decision is a useful noncontrolling lawful-vantage comparator for publicly visible police-facility areas from a public sidewalk. The 2026 Reyes decisions concern New York statutory/trespass issues and do not establish that a California police-lobby recording ban is constitutional.
