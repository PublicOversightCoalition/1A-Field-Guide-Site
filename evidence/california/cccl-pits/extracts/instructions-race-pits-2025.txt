Additional required field being added to PITS:
After much discussion, the Antiracism Committee has recommended, and Leadership has
decided to include racial descriptors in PITS. The following categories will be added as drop-
down options to the “Race” field in the “Perpetrator Info” section of PITS:

   •   Black/African American
   •   Asian
   •   Latina/o/x
   •   Middle Eastern and North African (MENA)
   •   Native American/Indigenous
   •   Pacific Islander
   •   South Asian
   •   No person visible (Phone call/chat/email)
   •   White

Instructions:
Staff reporting incidents must select the drop-down option based on their perception of the
perpetrator’s race. The field may be later updated and/or corrected if additional information
about how the person racially identifies becomes known. Staff should continue to use the free-
text description field to add nuance and details about the perpetrator’s physical description.
Why are we adding this field in now and how will we use this information?

Collecting data about race in incident reports serves two purposes:
   1. Help us to better identify people involved in incidents in our libraries
   2. Helps identify and address potential patterns within our responses to incidents. (For
      example, is Leadership and Management being consistent across racial groups in our
      suspension lengths or if additional antibias training needed.)
Data Use:
   •   Information will be tracked using the reporting features in PITS
   •   Data will not be used to draw conclusions about the criminality of any groups of library patrons
   •   Data may be used to inform future updates to Library policies, procedures, and practices
