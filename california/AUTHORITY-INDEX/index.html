    California Authority Index¶

    Purpose: Track every statewide/federal authority that may materially affect a peaceful First Amendment audit in a publicly accessible government area in California.

    This is a completeness ledger, not a claim that every listed authority creates a field rule.

    Status meanings¶

    • DOCTRINE-REVIEWED — controlling doctrine/material cases reviewed for the narrow proposition stated.
    • DOCTRINE-REVIEWED-IN-PART — narrow proposition reviewed; broader family/application incomplete.
    • PRIMARY-TEXT-VERIFIED — current primary text checked; case layer may remain incomplete.
    • PRIMARY-TEXT-VERIFIED-IN-PART — part of broader family checked.
    • SOURCE-CHECKED — primary text/source checked but handbook treatment incomplete.
    • RESEARCH — identified; not yet fully reviewed.
    • SPECIALIZED — distinct facility/event/context.
    • FACILITY-SPECIFIC — depends on particular agency/facility/local rule.
    • N/A-STANDING-SCOPE — reviewed but ordinarily outside the standing peaceful-audit facts.

    Remaining specialized research families¶

    The ordinary California qualification branches are covered by the matrix and Sweeps 011–018. The following work remains specialized, changed-fact, facility-specific, or remedies-oriented rather than an activation-blocking ordinary-branch Gap:

    • facility-specific government-building forum classifications and local rule sources;
    • Vehicle Code § 2806.5 edge questions beyond the ordinary pedestrian-stop notice branch;
    • force/arrest-conversion, collective-knowledge, mistake-of-law/fact, and full traffic-stop doctrine beyond the ordinary encounter tree;
    • the expressly Researched-Unresolved otherwise-lawful-detention affirmative-ID boundary;
    • the expressly Researched-Unresolved alphanumeric-passcode / foregone-conclusion edge;
    • specialized digital-warrant, cloud-provider, forensic-extraction, compelled-deletion, and CalECPA questions;
    • school/campus, K-12, courthouse, federal-premises, and other facility-specific access rules;
    • specialized CPRA exemption/retention families and local retention schedules;
    • independent California constitutional doctrines beyond the propositions necessary for the ordinary canonical branches;
    • §1983, Bane Act, qualified immunity, municipal liability, damages, suppression, discovery, and other remedies beyond the field-use escalation/preservation branch.

    Earlier sweep notes that described caller reliability, fighting words/true threats, plain feel, ordinary recording-device seizure, public-agency interference, emergency scenes, or ordinary law-enforcement records as "pending" have been superseded by Sweeps 014–018 and the final qualification review.

    Completeness rule¶

    Qualification means the overlay can responsibly run the canonical ordinary scenarios; it does not mean the handbook contains every California statute, every facility rule, or every remedies doctrine. Remaining specialized entries must stay visibly labeled rather than being mistaken for either completed ordinary doctrine or activation-blocking Gaps.
