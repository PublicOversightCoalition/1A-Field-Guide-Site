    California investigations¶

    California is the first Active jurisdiction in the 1A Field Guide. Investigations in this section inherit the California legal framework and then apply it to specific agencies, facilities, records systems, and incidents.

    This is the jurisdiction-level parent for California investigation files. The cross-jurisdiction browse surface remains Research & Investigations.

    Active¶

    Contra Costa County Library — Patron Incident Tracking System¶

    Status: ACTIVE · RECORDS PENDING
    Scope: Contra Costa County Library · Concord Library · Walnut Creek Library

    The investigation examines what PITS preserves about patron conduct, staff response, classification, suspension, deletion, and outside-agency involvement, and whether the records produced are complete enough to audit those uses.

    Relationship to the California handbook¶

    Investigation files do not replace the handbook. They consume it.

    A California investigation should link to the relevant statewide doctrine — public records, recording, police encounters, public forums, trespass/removal, privacy, or meetings — and then identify the local facts, policies, records, and unresolved questions that make the investigation specific.
