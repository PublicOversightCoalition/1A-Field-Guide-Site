Map-hardening research tasks

Imagery has substantially improved the physical model. These remaining tasks harden legal boundaries and government control.

Boundary evidence

Confirm sidewalk, entrance-approach and park connector boundaries

Locate the controlling City parcel/ROW/easement map or recorded dedication showing where Broadway/Lincoln sidewalks, library approaches and Civic Park circulation legally begin and end.

Site plan

Locate final as-built/site circulation plan

Use an official plan to replace imagery-derived edges with exact library footprint, public entrances, surface parking, underground garage access, Civic Park connections and pedestrian circulation.

Ownership/control

Resolve fenced private-sign edge

Determine the owner/controller of the fenced north end of the east parking area before assigning any public-forum label to that edge.

Agency position

Find any CCCL/City forum or solicitation guidance

Locate any formal legal memo, enforcement guidance, permit rule, training or correspondence stating how the City or County classifies exterior areas or applies solicitation/leafleting restrictions there.
