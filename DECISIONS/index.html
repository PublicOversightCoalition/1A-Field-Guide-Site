    Durable Decisions¶

    Use this file only for lasting project, research-governance, or architecture rationale that maintainers need and that is not already explained beside the authoritative source. GitHub Issues and Pull Requests own transient planning, status, implementation history, and review state.

    D-001 - California is the nationwide research reference implementation¶

    Decision: Nationwide expansion must preserve or strengthen the research rigor established by the California methodology. New states are not independent wikis with locally chosen evidence standards.

    Rationale: The value of the Field Guide depends on consistent treatment of authority, policy, evidence, uncertainty, and review across jurisdictions.

    D-002 - Contribution does not confer publication authority¶

    Decision: Coalition members may submit meaningful work without direct GitHub access. Contribution points may make a member eligible to request promotion but do not automatically grant roles, permissions, publication authority, or division leadership.

    Rationale: Contribution volume measures useful participation; elevated authority requires trust and human review.

    D-003 - Field Guide qualification precedes facility research¶

    Decision: All 50 states, the District of Columbia, and federal material may be represented from inception, but a jurisdiction's Field Guide/division qualification is separate from its facility-research maturity. A jurisdiction moves from Inactive to Field Guide Development to Active only through human qualification of its legal/research framework. Only after Active status may facility research progress from Unresearched to Researching to Established.

    Rationale: Facility conclusions require a qualified legal and methodological framework against which policies, access conditions, enforcement evidence, and incidents can be measured. California established that framework before using it for facility analysis. Separating the two status dimensions prevents facility activity or contribution counters from being mistaken for legal-framework readiness.

    D-004 - The Coalition platform mediates ordinary GitHub participation¶

    Decision: Ordinary coalition members do not require repository access. The platform accepts structured submissions and may prepare GitHub artifacts after approval. Repository publication and merge remain distinct human-controlled actions.

    Rationale: This preserves the existing review workflow, reduces credential/permission exposure, and allows contributors to participate without learning GitHub.

    D-005 - Public attribution may be institutional¶

    Decision: Internal provenance and accountability may coexist with public institutional/division attribution. The system does not require public disclosure of founder, administrator, requester, or contributor identity merely because associated research is published.

    Rationale: Public credibility should rest on transparent sources and methods while unnecessary personal exposure is minimized.

    D-006 - Common encounter scenarios belong in the national core¶

    Decision: Recurring encounters whose constitutional/factual structure is jurisdiction-neutral are modeled once in the canonical national core, with state/circuit overlays supplying offense elements and binding local authority. The complaint-driven disputed-trespass encounter is canonical scenario NC-COMPLAINT-01.

    Rationale: A complaint-driven police response has the same analytical spine nationwide—location, allegation, caller reliability, actual offense elements, Fourth Amendment justification, contradictory evidence, dissipation, disposition—even though state trespass and false-report laws differ. Modeling the encounter nationally prevents fifty inconsistent decision trees while preserving jurisdiction-specific law and authority hierarchy.

    D-007 - Founder may use a documented bootstrap activation exception¶

    Decision: Independent qualified human review remains the normal jurisdiction-activation path. When no independent qualified human reviewer is reasonably available, the Founder may activate a jurisdiction through the documented founding/bootstrap exception after all other substantive qualification requirements are satisfied and an enhanced review record is completed. The exception must be explicit and audited; it does not claim that independent review occurred, and AI/automation does not count as the missing independent human reviewer.

    Rationale: Early-stage governance must not make the project permanently unable to activate a thoroughly researched jurisdiction solely because no outside volunteer has yet become qualified and available. Keeping the exception explicit preserves accountability without fabricating independence. A bootstrap-activated jurisdiction remains Active unless later review identifies a material defect; later availability of an independent reviewer triggers retrospective review rather than automatic deactivation.

    D-008 - State activation creates county coverage inventory; counties inherit state qualification¶

    Decision: A state may qualify and become Active without completing research for every county. As part of the state activation transition, the project must create or validate a complete inventory of the state's counties or county-equivalents. County records use inherited local-research coverage statuses (Unresearched / Researching / Established) and do not receive independent state-style Field Guide qualification merely because they are county-level units.

    Rationale: Requiring complete county research before state activation would make nationwide qualification impractical and would duplicate national/circuit/state doctrine thousands of times. A complete county inventory still guarantees geographic scaffolding from the moment a state becomes Active, while local research can then proceed systematically under the qualified parent framework.

    D-009 - Lawful-vantage observation and conditional-access exclusion belong in the national core¶

    Decision: The national horizontal scaffold includes a canonical lawful-vantage/exposed-to-public-view scenario that applies whether the subject is government or private, and the complaint-driven trespass scenario separately preserves conditional-access/derivative-trespass analysis when continued access is conditioned on ceasing recording, speech, observation, or similar activity. Prospective no-entry notice is also separated from any independently applicable duty to identify.

    Rationale: These issues recur across states and facility types. Modeling them nationally prevents later trespass or confidentiality labels from erasing upstream questions about location, observation method, privacy/intrusion boundaries, restriction authority, exclusion validity, and identification authority. State overlays supply the actual privacy, trespass, recording, audio, exclusion, and identification law rather than importing California-specific answers.
