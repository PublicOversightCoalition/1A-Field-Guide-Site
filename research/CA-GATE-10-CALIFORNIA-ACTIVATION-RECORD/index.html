    Scope of activation¶

    California is now an Active jurisdiction overlay for Coalition facility analysis. This means the statewide legal/research framework has passed the project's qualification gate.

    Activation does not mean:

    • every California county has been locally researched;
    • every facility has been classified or established;
    • every future factual variation is already resolved;
    • every state/local policy is constitutionally valid;
    • the four Researched-Unresolved rows are resolved; or
    • California law governs federal premises merely because they are geographically inside California.

    Statewide facility-research maturity therefore begins at Unresearched.

    Bootstrap review obligation¶

    The CA-GATE-09 Founder/bootstrap record remains part of the activation provenance. If a suitably qualified independent human reviewer later becomes reasonably available, California remains queued for retrospective independent review under D-007. That later review does not automatically deactivate California; any material defect is handled on its merits.
