    Registry Classification Hardening — Four Pilot Facilities¶

    Purpose¶

    The first Registry preview correctly preserved uncertainty but displayed too much of it at the facility level. This pass separates:

    • forum classification;
    • confidence;
    • recording / activity rules; and
    • field verification.

    A facility is no longer marked overall unresolved merely because some subareas remain open.

    The project assigns a classification before field observation when the subarea's purpose and access are sufficiently established and controlling or close authority gives a reliable answer. Field observation remains pending only for facts that could actually change the result.

    Formal Registry labels¶

    Forum: TPF, LPF, NPF, NOT PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE, UNRESOLVED.

    Confidence: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW.

    Verification is separate: DOCUMENTARY, FIELD VERIFIED, FIELD CHECK PENDING.

    Hardened pilot results¶

    Concord Library¶

    Subarea Project classification Confidence Remaining issue
    Salvio / Parkside ordinary municipal sidewalks TPF HIGH Exact edge only if location disputed
    Public entrance / immediate east approach TPF HIGH Current signs / physical confirmation
    Civic Center connective pedestrian walkway TPF HIGH Exact subarea edge if disputed
    Front Salvio public lot TPF for lawful pedestrian expressive use HIGH Safety / vehicle rules remain
    Rear Parkside / Civic Center lot TPF MEDIUM Reserved zones / exact circulation
    Ordinary staffed interior LPF for library-purpose activity MEDIUM Recording is a separate activity question
    Concord meeting room LPF HIGH Current practice verification
    Staff / restricted spaces NOT PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE HIGH Exact door / boundary only

    The ordinary interior is no longer shown as wholly forum-unresolved. Prigmore expressly notes federal library-interior limited-public-forum authority, and Faith Center relies on the library's reading/writing/quiet-contemplation purpose. Confidence remains MEDIUM because the closest ordinary-interior holdings are persuasive rather than a Ninth Circuit merits decision classifying these exact general-use areas. That classification does not itself establish a right to film every interior subject.

    The garage classification is hardened federally because it is an enclosed, purpose-built vehicle-storage/circulation facility with no evidence of an expressive opening. California treatment remains unresolved because the state Speech Clause can diverge for freely accessible exterior spaces and no close garage authority was located.

    Concord DMV¶

    Subarea Project classification Confidence Remaining issue
    Actual Diamond / Meridian Park ordinary municipal sidewalk TPF HIGH Establish exact physical strip
    Facility-specific access drive / walk NPF — federal MEDIUM Current legal geometry/control
    Customer parking NPF — federal MEDIUM California treatment / exact use
    Exterior customer queue / immediate approach NPF — federal MEDIUM Exact footprint / California treatment
    Ordinary lobby / waiting NPF — federal HIGH California Speech Clause unresolved
    Customer service counters NPF — federal HIGH Privacy / exact restriction
    Knowledge-test stations NPF — federal HIGH Activity-specific device rule already verified
    Employee / secure areas NOT PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE HIGH Exact boundary only

    The federal lobby classification is hardened from “strongly supported” to NPF / HIGH as a project classification. Sammartano is published Ninth Circuit authority treating a public government-business complex as nonpublic; Freedom Foundation is a close, though unpublished, state-agency-lobby comparator. No exact California DMV filming holding is implied.

    Concord Police Headquarters¶

    Subarea Project classification Confidence Remaining issue
    Ordinary Galindo municipal sidewalk TPF HIGH Exact physical strip if disputed
    Facility-controlled front approach / parking NPF — federal MEDIUM Confirm footprint/control
    Ordinary public lobby / Community Service Desk NPF — federal HIGH California Speech Clause unresolved
    Records counter NPF — federal HIGH Privacy / confidentiality / actual rule
    Community Meeting Room LPF HIGH Contract details / current practice
    Controlled / operational areas NOT PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE HIGH Exact boundaries

    The ordinary lobby is hardened to NPF / HIGH federally because its documented purpose is transactional police service, not public discourse, and Sammartano supplies controlling municipal-building doctrine. The recording analysis remains separate.

    For an officer performing public-facing official duties in the lobby, the current activity-specific record is unusually strong: CPD Policy 423 recognizes lawful public recording of officers, Penal Code § 148(g) prevents recording alone from becoming § 148(a) obstruction or the sole basis for reasonable suspicion/probable cause when its location conditions are met, and Wilson v. County of Contra Costa is a close local sheriff-lobby comparator. This does not create a categorical right to record victims, witnesses, confidential screens, private reports, or secure operations.

    What remains genuinely unresolved¶

    The hardening pass intentionally leaves several issues open:

    • California Speech Clause classification of ordinary DMV and police-service lobbies;
    • exact Diamond Boulevard DMV ingress legal geometry;
    • some facility-controlled approach/parking boundaries;
    • the Walnut Creek garage under California law;
    • recording of privacy-sensitive subjects and communications;
    • any current physical sign or rule not found online;
    • site-specific removal/trespass analysis without an actual direction, rule, authority and location.

    Those are narrower uncertainties. They no longer justify labeling the four facilities “overall unresolved.”
