    Walnut Creek Library — Policy Search Review¶

    Reviewed: 2026-09-22

    Sources reviewed¶

    Current CCCL public-facing sources reviewed for Walnut Creek-specific access/recording/room rules include:

    • branch page: https://ccclib.org/locations/26/
    • CCCL policy portal: https://ccclib.org/policies/
    • current Patron Conduct Policy linked from the policy portal;
    • CCCL Rooms directory: https://ccclib.org/rooms/
    • Las Trampas policy: https://ccclib.org/las-trampas-room-policy/
    • Group Study Room rules: https://ccclib.org/walnut-creek-library-group-study-room-rules/
    • system Room Use PDF revised April 2025: https://ccclib.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/72/2026/03/LP-RMS-MeetingRoomRules_2025E_ADA.pdf
    • City Oak View page: https://www.walnutcreekartsrec.org/parks-facilities/facility-rentals/walnut-creek-library-oak-view-room

    Targeted site searches for Walnut Creek + photography, video, filming, camera and recording did not locate a dedicated ordinary-patron camera rule.

    Photography / videography finding¶

    Approved wording:

    No dedicated public-facing Walnut Creek-specific ordinary-patron photography/videography rule was located in the online sources reviewed as of September 22, 2026.

    Do not shorten this to there is no photography policy.

    The finding does not establish:

    • absence of internal staff/security guidance;
    • absence of signs not reproduced online;
    • absence of program-specific or rental-specific media conditions;
    • absence of privacy or conduct rules applicable to particular conduct;
    • absence of a later-adopted policy.

    Current room-source discrepancy¶

    The 2025-revised system Room Use PDF says arrangements for both Oak View and Las Trampas are through City Facility Rentals.

    Current live sources now split the channels:

    • Oak View: City Facility Rentals;
    • Las Trampas: direct CCCL/online booking.

    The current City Oak View page expressly says smaller rooms such as Las Trampas are reserved directly through CCCL and are not facilitated by the City rental office. Current CCCL Rooms/Las Trampas pages agree.

    Working treatment:

    CURRENT-LIVE-SOURCES-SUPPORT-CCCL-LAS-TRAMPAS-BOOKING / OLDER-PDF-DISCREPANCY-PRESERVED

    The older PDF remains relevant history and could matter if a formal underlying control agreement is later disputed, but it should not silently override the newer mutually corroborating current-use sources.

    Study-room expression rule¶

    Current Walnut Creek Group Study Room rules expressly prohibit:

    • advertising;
    • petitions;
    • solicitations;
    • sales.

    This rule belongs to the study-room channel and must not be generalized to the ordinary Library interior, meeting rooms, civic plaza, sidewalks, park, or parking areas.

    Its constitutional validity has not been finally adjudicated by this branch sweep. The room remains a purpose-limited channel requiring its own forum/rule analysis.
