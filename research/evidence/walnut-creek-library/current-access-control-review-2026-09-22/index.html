    Walnut Creek Library — Current Access / Control Review¶

    Review date: 2026-09-22
    Purpose: Preserve current official-source facts that materially affect the area-specific forum/control analysis after the parcel pass.

    Library and parking¶

    Official CCCL branch page:

    https://ccclib.org/locations/26/

    Current public facts reviewed:

    • Walnut Creek Library — 1644 N. Broadway;
    • staffed hours Monday–Thursday 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday–Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., Sunday closed;
    • Library underground garage — 120 spaces, four-hour limit;
    • Library surface lot — 20 four-hour spaces and 10 thirty-minute spaces;
    • nearby Broadway Garage — separate downtown parking at 1390 N. Broadway.

    A September 2026 City Manager update independently identifies the Downtown Walnut Creek Library at 1644 N. Broadway and states that parking is available on site or at the nearby Broadway Garage.

    Source:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/Home/Components/News/News/3966/17

    The Broadway Garage is not treated as Library-property evidence merely because it is offered as nearby parking.

    Underground Library garage — current access facts¶

    The current City Oak View rental page states that the Library underground parking is metered and remains accessible when the Library is closed for after-hours Oak View events.

    Source:

    https://www.walnutcreekartsrec.org/parks-facilities/facility-rentals/walnut-creek-library-oak-view-room

    The City's sustainability materials independently identify public EV chargers at the Walnut Creek Library.

    These facts strengthen the conclusion that the garage is intentionally open to members of the public rather than an employee-only/security garage. They still do not establish traditional-public-forum status. The garage remains an enclosed special-purpose parking/circulation facility requiring its own forum analysis.

    Civic Park¶

    Current City park directory:

    https://www.walnutcreekartsrec.org/Home/Components/FacilityDirectory/FacilityDirectory/45/1920

    Current facts include:

    • address: 1375 Civic Drive;
    • 16.7 acres;
    • open dawn to dusk;
    • Community Center;
    • Library;
    • parking;
    • picnic area;
    • restrooms;
    • trail connections;
    • connections to the Iron Horse Trail and Creek Walk.

    A City publication separately states that Civic Park is open approximately 30 minutes before sunrise until 30 minutes after dusk, subject to special evening events.

    Source:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/30891/638418828841230000

    These sources support the public-park character and ordinary-access baseline. Specific reserved events, closures, permits, and generally applicable park rules remain separately enforceable where valid.

    Oak View Room — current City control¶

    Current City page:

    https://www.walnutcreekartsrec.org/parks-facilities/facility-rentals/walnut-creek-library-oak-view-room

    The City currently presents Oak View as a rentable second-floor room for meetings, corporate events, trainings, book signings, dinners and social events. Current features include:

    • separate after-hours entrance;
    • access when the rest of the Library is closed;
    • City Facility Rentals inquiry/application/contract process;
    • Library programming priority during Library operating hours;
    • social events limited to periods when the Library is closed;
    • underground parking accessible for after-hours events.

    The City page also expressly states that smaller rooms such as Las Trampas are reserved directly through the Contra Costa County Library website and are not facilitated by the City rental office.

    This is the strongest current public source located for present Oak View/Las Trampas booking control.

    Las Trampas control discrepancy¶

    Current live CCCL room sources provide direct CCCL/online reservation for Las Trampas:

    • https://ccclib.org/las-trampas-room-policy/
    • https://ccclib.org/rooms/

    An older system Room Use PDF revised in 2025 states that arrangements for both Oak View and Las Trampas are made through City of Walnut Creek Facility Rentals:

    https://ccclib.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/72/2026/03/LP-RMS-MeetingRoomRules_2025E_ADA.pdf

    The current City Oak View page now affirmatively states the opposite for smaller rooms such as Las Trampas: direct CCCL reservation, not City Facility Rentals.

    Working treatment:

    CURRENT-LIVE-SOURCES-SUPPORT-CCCL-LAS-TRAMPAS-BOOKING / OLDER-PDF-DISCREPANCY-PRESERVED

    This does not necessarily establish every underlying property/control allocation. It does establish that the project's current-use description should follow the newer mutually corroborating live City/CCCL sources while preserving the older conflicting document for history and any later authority question.

    Current parking-system distinction¶

    City downtown parking page:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/community/what-s-happening/parking-downtown

    The City separately describes its three downtown City garages — Broadway, Lesher Center and South Locust — as 24-hour public garages. That page also notes public EV chargers for visitors to the Library and City Hall.

    The underground Library garage is therefore not silently conflated with the City's Broadway Garage. Different access rules, pricing, operating purposes and forum facts may apply.

    Current engineering / ROW evidence family¶

    Walnut Creek Encroachment Permits:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/government/public-works/engineering-services/encroachment-permits

    Walnut Creek Site Plans and Diagrams:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/government/community-development-department/permits/building-permits/site-plans-and-diagrams

    The current site-plan requirements specifically call for property boundaries/easements and adjacent streets/public rights-of-way. The City also maintains official building records/plans and provides a records-request process:

    https://www.walnutcreekca.gov/government/community-development-department/permits/building-permits/request-building-records

    This identifies the next high-value current-boundary source family. If the exact Broadway/Lincoln/plaza/garage edge becomes outcome-determinative, current City building/engineering plans are a better next step than indiscriminately retrieving century-old subdivision maps.
