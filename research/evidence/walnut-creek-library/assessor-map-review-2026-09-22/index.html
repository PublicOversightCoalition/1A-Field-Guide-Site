    Walnut Creek Library — Assessor Map Review¶

    Source: Contra Costa County Assessor Map, Book 178 Page 30
    Official PDF: https://ccmap.cccounty.us/HTML5/assessorPDF/178p30x0.pdf
    Retrieved: 2026-09-22
    SHA-256: 0480ff4b000aa6593dd85600c4e6165a9359ed5ac3dd42c6c6765b90b84e99da

    Review status¶

    HUMAN-REVIEWED / SOURCE-LIMITED

    The sheet was retrieved automatically by Facility Prep and then visually/textually reviewed. It is assessor evidence, not a survey-grade legal-boundary determination.

    Target parcel observation¶

    The sheet depicts parcel 39 in the North Broadway / Lincoln Avenue / Civic Drive block and shows:

    • CITY OF WALNUT CREEK;
    • 2.202 Ac;
    • parcel number 39;
    • North Broadway along the west/southwest civic frontage;
    • Lincoln Avenue along the north side of the relevant block context;
    • direct relationship to adjoining parcel 40.

    This independently corresponds to the official GIS result for APN 178-300-039, 1644 Broadway.

    Adjoining City parcel¶

    The sheet depicts parcel 40 immediately adjoining parcel 39 and shows:

    • CITY OF WALNUT CREEK;
    • 7.548 Ac;
    • parcel number 40.

    The current GIS intersection query independently identifies APN 178-300-040, 1375 CIVIC DR, owner field WALNUT CREEK CITY OF, 7.548 acres. City materials place Civic Park Community Center at 1375 Civic Drive. Together these are strong evidence that parcel 40 is the adjoining City civic/park parcel, while exact subarea lines and control remain separate questions.

    Other visible context¶

    The sheet also depicts:

    • a City-owned parcel 37, approximately 2.0 Ac, south/across North Broadway;
    • North Main Street;
    • Civic Drive;
    • Carlback Avenue;
    • assessor page continuation reference TO PAGE 36;
    • map footer references FM 19-25, 20-29.

    The presence of another City parcel across the street is useful civic-campus context but does not make the intervening ROW, sidewalks, or every paved area one legal parcel/forum.

    Historical map references¶

    The sheet prints two subdivision-chain references:

    • RANCHO DOLORES SUB'N MB 2-36 7/26/1909
    • OAK PARK SUB'N MB 18-385 3/4/1922

    Both references were followed into Contra Costa Public Works' Recorded Maps index. The index independently returns matching Walnut Creek subdivision records; see recorded-map-review-2026-09-22.md.

    These historical maps are chain/context evidence. They are not automatically the controlling modern parcel or ROW survey.

    Official Records notation¶

    The sheet contains:

    NE COR. LEASE 1217 OR 76

    Its precise location/application is not clear enough from this review to assign the notation to parcel 39 or to infer a lease affecting the Library. It remains a record-reference lead only unless later evidence makes it outcome-determinative.

    Source disclaimer¶

    The assessor sheet expressly states that it was prepared for assessment purposes only, assumes no liability for the accuracy of the delineated information, and warns that assessor parcels may not comply with local lot-split or building-site ordinances.

    Accordingly:

    • the sheet strongly corroborates parcel identity and relative parcel arrangement;
    • it does not establish a survey-grade legal boundary;
    • it does not establish the exact North Broadway or Lincoln Avenue ROW edge;
    • it does not establish every easement or operational-control allocation;
    • it does not itself establish constitutional forum classification.

    Research consequence¶

    Unlike Concord, the Walnut Creek sheet immediately reveals a denser mixed civic geometry: the Library parcel directly adjoins a much larger City civic/park parcel and the City's historical design materials describe multiple entrances, outdoor plazas, surface parking, and an underground garage. The next legal analysis therefore must distinguish those specific exterior circulation areas rather than treat the 2.202-acre assessor parcel as one forum.
