    Concord DMV — Assessor Map Review¶

    Source: Contra Costa County Assessor Map, Book 126 Page 49
    Official PDF: https://ccmap.cccounty.us/HTML5/assessorPDF/126p49x0.pdf
    Retrieved: 2026-09-22
    SHA-256: d3e6dc5d5f889a5d8ab368c554c1b41d3573e68acc5e539be30584637a3e426c

    Review status¶

    HUMAN-REVIEWED / SOURCE-LIMITED

    The map is image-only for ordinary PDF text extraction and was visually reviewed. It is assessor evidence, not a survey-grade legal-boundary determination.

    Target parcel¶

    Official GIS resolves the DMV as APN 126-490-004, owner field CALIFORNIA STATE OF, second owner/name field DEPT MOTOR VEHICLES.

    The assessor sheet depicts parcel 04 within:

    TRACT 5292 (WILLOWICK 4) MB 212-47

    and shows approximately 3.49 Ac inside parcel 04.

    The sheet's relative geometry shows parcel 04:

    • east of parcel 01;
    • north of parcel 03;
    • south/southwest of parcel 05;
    • directly adjoining the west side of Meridian Park Boulevard;
    • not visually adjoining Diamond Boulevard on this assessor depiction.

    Address / frontage mismatch¶

    DMV and the current GIS both identify the facility address as:

    2070 Diamond Boulevard

    But Book 126 Page 49 depicts parcel 01 between parcel 04 and Diamond Boulevard.

    That creates a material research flag:

    ADDRESS/FRONTAGE-MISMATCH / CURRENT-PUBLIC-ACCESS-PATH-UNRESOLVED

    The map does not establish why the facility carries a Diamond Boulevard address. Possible explanations could include a driveway/access easement, shared access, a site-plan configuration, or some other historical/current arrangement. No particular explanation is adopted without the underlying record.

    This matters because a future exclusion/trespass dispute at an access drive or sidewalk could turn on whether the person is on:

    • ordinary Concord street/sidewalk ROW;
    • the State parcel;
    • an access easement across private property;
    • a shared driveway;
    • another separately controlled parcel.

    Surrounding parcels / streets¶

    The sheet also shows:

    • parcel 01 immediately west of 04, fronting Diamond Boulevard;
    • parcel 02 south of parcel 01 and fronting Diamond Boulevard;
    • parcel 03 south of parcel 04 and fronting Meridian Park Boulevard;
    • parcel 05 north/northeast of parcel 04 near Galaxy Way / Meridian Park Boulevard;
    • Diamond Boulevard along the west side of the tract;
    • Galaxy Way along the north side;
    • Meridian Park Boulevard along the east side.

    The current GIS candidate-neighbor results independently identify parcels 01, 02, 03 and 05 as separate current parcels with separate owners.

    Recorded-map lead¶

    The exact tract reference printed on the assessor sheet is:

    TRACT 5292 (WILLOWICK 4) MB 212-47

    Contra Costa Public Works' public Recorded Maps index independently resolves that reference to:

    • 212M47;
    • entry ID 148419;
    • Subdivision Map;
    • recorded June 28, 1978;
    • permit SD78-05292;
    • subdivision name WILLOWICK #4;
    • three imaged pages.

    The public scan is therefore the next proper historical/access-geometry record to review.

    Source limitation¶

    The assessor sheet expressly states it was prepared for assessment purposes and disclaims guaranteed accuracy.

    Accordingly, it strongly supports parcel identity and relative parcel arrangement but does not by itself establish:

    • the current legal access easement;
    • exact public ROW edges;
    • current driveway ownership/control;
    • every dedication/easement;
    • constitutional forum classification.
