    Concord Library — Assessor Map Review¶

    Facility: Concord Library
    Address: 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, California 94519
    Target APN: 111-240-006
    Adjacent Civic Center candidate: 111-240-014
    Assessor sheet: Book 111, Page 24
    Source: https://ccmap.cccounty.us/HTML5/assessorPDF/111p24x0.pdf
    Facility Prep retrieval: 2026-09-22
    Preserved source SHA-256: ba30d406493dcda4b1f1ef92759d155a879062c2595614711f90fbfd8966c62d

    Review status¶

    This is a human visual review of the official Contra Costa County assessor-map sheet preserved by Facility Prep v0.2. The PDF contains no usable extractable text through the current parser, so the map was reviewed visually.

    This sheet is evidence of assessor parcel geometry and record-reference notation. It is not treated as a survey, deed, final right-of-way determination, or complete easement report.

    Reviewed observations¶

    1. Parcel 06 on Assessor Book 111 Page 24 is labeled LIBRARY, approximately 0.84 acres. The sheet prints 3356 OR 502 beneath the LIBRARY label.
    2. Parcel 14 is immediately east of parcel 06 and is labeled CITY OF CONCORD, approximately 3.68 acres. The sheet prints 636 OR 390 beneath that label.
    3. Parcel 06 directly fronts Salvio Street on the north and lies immediately east of Parkside Drive.
    4. Parcel 14 also fronts Salvio Street on the north and continues substantially farther south/east than the library parcel.
    5. The Salvio Street corridor is drawn with 40 dimension callouts on each side of the depicted centerline. This is a useful right-of-way research lead but is not treated as a final legal ROW determination without engineering/recorded-source confirmation.
    6. Near the north end of Parkside Drive, the sheet shows a 50' dimension and an additional narrow-line/dimension treatment adjacent to parcel 06. The exact legal meaning of those lines must be resolved from recorded or engineering sources rather than inferred from the assessor sheet alone.
    7. The sheet revision table includes a 10/10/96 entry stating CREATE 013 & 014.
    8. The sheet contains additional historic easement/right-of-way notation along Willow Pass Road, including a permanent-easement notation, but those southern references are not presently treated as establishing rights affecting the Concord Library parcel.

    Official-record notation convention¶

    The OR abbreviation can now be interpreted with substantially greater confidence because other official Contra Costa County-hosted records expressly pair a full citation with the same shorthand convention.

    For example, a County-hosted legal description states that a deed was recorded in Book 1396 of Official Records, Page 312, then later refers to the same parcel as (1396 OR 312):

    https://www.contracosta.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/73288/Appendix-F---Hazards-Hazardous-Materials-and-Wildfire-Supporting-Info-PDF

    Another County-hosted title/legal-description record uses the same pattern, including shorthand such as (535 OR 213) after describing records by Book and Page of Official Records:

    https://www.contracosta.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/52264/Appendix-M---Phase-I-Environmental-Site-Assessment-PDF

    Accordingly, the assessor-sheet notations are now reasonably read as:

    • 3356 OR 502 → Book 3356 of Official Records, Page 502;
    • 636 OR 390 → Book 636 of Official Records, Page 390.

    This resolves the citation format, not the content or legal effect of either instrument. The underlying records still need to be retrieved before the project states what they conveyed, reserved, dedicated, or otherwise established.

    Candidate recorded-record references¶

    The following should be pursued as recorded-record research targets:

    • parcel 06 / Library: Book 3356 of Official Records, Page 502;
    • parcel 14 / City of Concord: Book 636 of Official Records, Page 390;
    • assessor sheet: Book 111, Page 24;
    • sheet revision: 10/10/96 — CREATE 013 & 014.

    Contra Costa RecorderWorks exposes an Old Book workflow in addition to newer Book/Page searching, so these older Official Records references should be attempted there or through the Clerk-Recorder's equivalent record-retrieval process:

    https://crsecurepayment.com/RW/?ln=en

    Boundary consequence for Sweep 007¶

    The assessor map materially improves the exterior analysis:

    • the library building parcel and the larger Civic Center parcel are separate assessor parcels;
    • both are currently returned by Concord GIS with the owner field CONCORD CITY OF;
    • parcel 06 and parcel 14 are adjacent;
    • the street-facing geometry is now substantially better documented than it was from the public-facing Civic Center map alone;
    • the printed Official Records references for both parcels have now been translated into reproducible Book/Page research targets.

    Still unresolved:

    • the contents and legal effect of Book 3356 OR Page 502 and Book 636 OR Page 390;
    • deed/title corroboration;
    • exact legal Salvio Street right-of-way;
    • exact legal Parkside Drive right-of-way;
    • any public-access, utility, reciprocal-access, or other easements affecting the relevant approaches;
    • whether front/rear parking areas and entrance walks fall wholly within parcel 06, parcel 14, street ROW, or a combination;
    • current City/County operational-control agreements;
    • the constitutional forum classification of each resulting physical area.
