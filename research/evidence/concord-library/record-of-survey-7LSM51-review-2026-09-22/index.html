    Concord Library — Record of Survey 7 LSM 51 Review¶

    Facility: Concord Library, 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, CA 94519
    Reviewed: 2026-09-22
    Official record: Contra Costa County Public Works Laserfiche, Record of Survey Map 7LSM51, entry 126622
    Recorded: 1940-09-24
    Surveyor metadata: C. W. Gibbs
    APN-book metadata: 111 24
    Status: HISTORICAL-SURVEY-VERIFIED / CURRENT-BOUNDARY-EFFECT-UNRESOLVED

    Acquisition¶

    Facility Prep resolved the assessor-map annotation 7 LSM 51 through the County Public Works Recorded Maps search. The public search returned entry 126622, name 7LSM51, record type Record of Survey Map, one imaged page, APN metadata 111 24, and a recording date of September 24, 1940.

    The entry is an imaged Laserfiche document rather than an electronic PDF. The ordinary anonymous ElectronicFile.aspx route therefore reports an empty electronic file, but the public document viewer exposes the scanned page. Facility Prep reproduced that ordinary public rendering route and preserved a rendered page in the review artifact.

    No authentication, CAPTCHA bypass, access-control bypass, paid transaction, or nonpublic endpoint was used.

    Human-reviewed map observations¶

    The rendered survey sheet states that it depicts:

    A portion of the property of the Concord Home Acres Company.

    It identifies that company's deed as Book 454 of Official Records, Page 154, and states that the property is located in Rancho Monte del Diablo.

    The survey further states that the course of Salvio Street, as extended, was taken as the basis for bearings. The drawing depicts a 10.60-acre tract and shows:

    • the Sacramento Northern Railway along the west side;
    • Willow Pass Road along the south side, with directional notes toward Concord and Pittsburg;
    • a line labeled as the centerline/course of Salvio Street as extended;
    • iron-bar and iron-pipe monument references;
    • survey bearings and distances around the depicted tract.

    The sheet is dated June 1940 and shows County approval/filing in September 1940.

    What this record supports¶

    7LSM51 is verified historical survey evidence tied by County metadata to assessor Book 111 Page 24. It provides a contemporaneous surveyed relationship among the larger historical tract, Salvio Street, Willow Pass Road, and the former Sacramento Northern Railway corridor.

    It also supplies a new underlying-record lead: 454 OR 154.

    What this record does not yet establish¶

    The survey predates the present Concord Library building, the current parcel configuration, and the assessor-sheet revision that created parcels 013 and 014 in 1996. It therefore is not treated as the current legal boundary of APN 111-240-006 or 111-240-014.

    The sheet does not by itself establish:

    • the current Salvio Street right-of-way edge at the library;
    • the current Parkside Drive right-of-way edge;
    • title to a particular modern sidewalk strip;
    • the boundaries of the current front or rear parking areas;
    • current easements, dedications, or access rights;
    • current City/County operational-control allocation;
    • constitutional forum status.

    Those questions remain dependent on later/current recorded maps, engineering/dedication material, title/control records, and physical-area overlay.

    Follow-up¶

    1. Resolve Book 454 OR Page 154 through the County Recorder index and determine whether the underlying instrument is publicly viewable or requires manual copy acquisition.
    2. Search the County Public Works Recorded Maps collection by APN/assessor-book reference 111 24, not merely by the single assessor annotation, to identify later subdivision, parcel-map, survey, or corner-record material affecting the current parcels.
    3. Prioritize later/current records over this 1940 survey when determining modern parcel and right-of-way edges.
    4. Preserve 7LSM51 as historical geometry/provenance evidence even if later records supersede its practical boundary value.
