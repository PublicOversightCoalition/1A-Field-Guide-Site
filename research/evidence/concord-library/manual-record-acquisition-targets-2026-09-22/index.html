    Concord Library — Manual Recorded-Instrument Acquisition Targets¶

    Facility: Concord Library, 2900 Salvio Street, Concord, CA 94519
    Prepared: 2026-09-22
    Status: OFFICIAL-IDENTIFIERS-RESOLVED / IMAGES-NOT-AVAILABLE-ONLINE

    Facility Prep resolved the following historical Official Records references through Contra Costa County RecorderWorks using the public anonymous Old Book Display Document workflow. For each target, the public viewer recognizes a specific document identifier but does not expose the recorded image online.

    Current portal result¶

    The ordinary RecorderWorks ImagePresentor response for these records states that images may not be viewed online. Under the Facility Prep SOP, automated acquisition stops there.

    A resolved document ID establishes that the public index recognized the supplied book/page reference. It does not establish the instrument type, parties, date, legal description, easements, dedications, title effect, or present boundary consequence.

    Acquisition priority¶

    If manual copies become necessary, prioritize:

    1. 3356 OR 502 — highest priority because it is printed directly on the current assessor parcel 06 / Library area.
    2. 636 OR 390 — high priority because it is printed on adjacent current parcel 14 / Civic Center area.
    3. 454 OR 154 — historical-chain record; useful for provenance and interpreting 7LSM51, but less likely by itself to resolve the modern 1996+ parcel/ROW configuration.

    Ordinary research copies are sufficient unless a later legal question specifically requires a certified copy.

    Questions the copies may answer¶

    For each instrument, review for:

    • grantor/grantee or other parties;
    • instrument type and recording date;
    • legal description and parcel geometry;
    • street dedications or reservations;
    • easements or rights-of-way;
    • references to subdivision maps, parcel maps, surveys, or later instruments;
    • language affecting City/County ownership or control;
    • whether the instrument predates or explains the 1996 creation of assessor parcels 013 and 014.

    Do not infer modern forum status from any deed or recorded instrument without separately analyzing present public access, control, use, and applicable First Amendment doctrine.
