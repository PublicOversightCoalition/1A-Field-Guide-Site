    Facility Registry¶

    Data-driven working Registry. One compact row per facility. Forum classification, confidence, recording posture, and field verification are separate fields so a pending site visit does not make an otherwise answerable legal question look unresolved.

    Forum labels below are project classifications, not claims that a court has adjudicated that exact address.

    Constitution First: a government rule is recorded as a claimed restriction until its legal and constitutional support is analyzed. RULE LOCATED never means lawful prohibition by itself.

    TPF traditional public forum LPF limited public forum NPF nonpublic forum UNRESOLVED a real gap could change the answer HIGH strong classification confidence MEDIUM defensible but more fact-sensitive FIELD PENDING verification only unless stated otherwise

    Concord DMV Field Office¶

    2070 Diamond Boulevard, Concord
    State Administrative Service · California Department of Motor Vehicles

    CA-013-F0003

    Key classifications

    TPF · HIGH actual municipal sidewalks
    NPF · HIGH lobby/waiting · transaction counters · testing channel federal
    NPF · MEDIUM customer parking · facility-specific approach/queue federal

    Recording: Recording can constitute First Amendment-protected activity. No dedicated public-facing California DMV field-office ordinary-patron camera prohibition was located online. The knowledge-test phone rule is activity-specific and cannot be generalized into a branch-wide camera ban; any broader restriction requires its own legal and constitutional basis.

    CA QUESTIONS REMAIN INDOOR ANALYZED FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Actual Diamond ingress route and legal/control geometry; Current parking and exterior queue geometry; Entrance signs.

    Concord Library¶

    2900 Salvio Street, Concord
    Public Library · Contra Costa County Library

    CA-013-F0001

    Key classifications

    TPF · HIGH sidewalks · entrance/approach · civic walkway · front lot
    LPF · MEDIUM ordinary interior for library-purpose activity
    LPF · HIGH meeting room
    TPF · MEDIUM rear Civic Center lot

    Recording: Recording can constitute First Amendment-protected activity. No dedicated public-facing Concord-specific ordinary-patron camera prohibition was located online. The library-purpose forum classification does not itself create a camera ban; any restriction must be tied to a valid privacy, disruption, safety, or other legal interest and constitutionally tested.

    CA QUESTIONS REMAIN INDOOR ANALYZED FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Current entrance and forecourt signs; Rear-lot reserved or employee-only zones; Exact ROW edge only where enforcement turns on a few feet.

    Concord Police Headquarters¶

    1350 Galindo Street, Concord
    Police Headquarters · City of Concord Police Department

    CA-013-F0004

    Key classifications

    TPF · HIGH ordinary Galindo municipal sidewalk
    NPF · HIGH public lobby / Community Service Desk · Records counter federal
    LPF · HIGH Community Meeting Room

    Recording: Recording public-facing police activity from a lawful, noninterfering vantage retains strong activity-specific support from CPD Policy 423, Penal Code §148(g), Fordyce, Askins, Project Veritas, and the local Wilson comparator. The 2026 Second Circuit Massimino decision is a useful noncontrolling lawful-vantage comparator for publicly visible police-facility areas from a public sidewalk. The 2026 Reyes decisions concern New York statutory/trespass issues and do not establish that a California police-lobby recording ban is constitutional.

    CA QUESTIONS REMAIN INDOOR ANALYZED FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Current entrance/lobby signs; Counter privacy configuration; Controlled-door boundaries.

    Healdsburg City Hall / Council Chamber¶

    401 Grove Street, Healdsburg
    City Hall · City of Healdsburg

    CA-097-F0002

    Key classifications

    LPF · HIGH covered open-meeting channel
    NPF · MEDIUM public service lobby
    UNRESOLVED chamber outside open meetings

    Recording: Covered Brown Act meetings have statutory recording protection subject to the disruption standard. That meeting-specific rule does not determine recording in service counters, hallways, closed sessions, or the chamber outside an open meeting.

    CA QUESTIONS REMAIN FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Record public entry/counter layout, posted hours, visitor and recording signs, and controlled-area transitions.; For a scheduled open meeting, preserve the agenda, posted comment/recording procedures, room layout, and actual access conditions.; Verify the Grove Street sidewalk, entry approach, and parking/right-of-way transitions against official property records..

    Healdsburg Plaza¶

    Matheson Street and Healdsburg Avenue, Healdsburg
    Public Park · City of Healdsburg Community Services

    CA-097-F0004

    Key classifications

    TPF · HIGH ordinary public park
    TPF · HIGH gazebo / performance conditions
    TPF · HIGH ordinary municipal sidewalks

    Recording: Ordinary public-place recording and the conditions attached to a performance permit require separate treatment. Resolution 116-2023 includes a commercial-film-permit condition for permit holders; the complete film-permit framework and its reach to ordinary visitors have not been established.

    FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Document posted park/performance/film signs, the gazebo, ordinary pedestrian paths, and event occupation without inferring a general closure.; Identify municipal sidewalk and curb boundaries separately from park paths, roadways, adjoining businesses, and event footprints.; Preserve current permit criteria and any actual official direction; document the activity, exact location, stated rule, and response..

    Healdsburg Police Department¶

    238 Center Street, Healdsburg
    Police Headquarters · City of Healdsburg Police Department

    CA-097-F0001

    Key classifications

    TPF · HIGH ordinary municipal sidewalk
    NPF · MEDIUM public police-service lobby
    UNRESOLVED approach / parking boundaries

    Recording: Policy 463 recognizes lawful recording of officer activity. That protection, lobby access, third-party privacy, and any actual restriction require separate analysis. Policies 1045.2.1 and 1045.2.3 separately address requested ID-card display and uniform identification; neither is a public filming ban.

    CA QUESTIONS REMAIN FIELD PENDING

    Main open field items: Document posted lobby hours and reconcile the complaint page’s 24/7 form-access statement with the daily 7:30 a.m.–7:00 p.m. lobby schedule.; Record public entrance, accessible complaint/records information, current camera/phone signs, and where controlled doors begin.; Distinguish the street-side sidewalk from the facility approach, driveway, public parking, and secure vehicle areas; obtain maps for disputed boundaries..

    Scale model¶

    The compact index is designed to remain usable when the Registry grows from four facilities to counties and eventually statewide coverage. The structured records now also emit registry.json and a flat registry.csv, which are the foundation for later client-side search, filters, pagination, maps, and filtered downloads.

    The source of truth is registry/data/facilities/*.json; these Markdown pages are generated outputs.
