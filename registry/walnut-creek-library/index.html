    Indoor common-space / privacy responsibility¶

    These rows separate a facility's privacy/confidentiality duties from duties that actually apply to a visitor. A privacy interest can support a narrower restriction, but it does not become a blanket camera ban merely by being asserted.

    Read the indoor common-space doctrine.

    Area Space type Privacy context HIPAA posture Facility responsibility Visitor responsibility Recording consequence
    Ordinary lobby, stacks, reading and service areas General Shared Interior Routine Sensitive Information Possible Not Indicated The library retains responsibility for safeguarding patron records, account information, staff systems, and other confidential material under laws and policies that apply to the library. A lawful visitor does not assume the library's confidentiality or information-security duties merely by being present. Any visitor duty must arise from law or a constitutionally valid rule that applies directly. Privacy around screens, transactions, or conversations may justify targeted restrictions; it does not itself establish a general camera prohibition in the ordinary public interior.
    Garage stairs, elevators and connective corridors Shared Corridor No Special Risk Identified Not Indicated The facility remains responsible for any protected information or secure operations it exposes to this public circulation route. A lawful visitor does not acquire facility confidentiality duties merely by using the public circulation route; independent law may still regulate particular conduct. No special privacy rationale is established in the current record. Any claimed recording restriction must be identified and tested under the NPF standard and applicable recording law.

    Recording posture¶

    Recording can constitute First Amendment-protected activity. No dedicated public-facing Walnut Creek-specific ordinary-patron camera prohibition was located online. The library-purpose forum classification does not itself create a camera ban; any restriction must be separately sourced and constitutionally tested.
