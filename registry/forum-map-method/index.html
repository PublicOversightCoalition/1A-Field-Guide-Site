    Facility Forum Map standard¶

    A facility is not field-ready merely because the Registry contains a general forum label. The Field Guide must identify the meaningful physical zones a lawful visitor can encounter and provide a supported First Amendment forum determination for each zone.

    Constitution-first rule¶

    Government ownership, a posted rule, or an agency's own forum label does not by itself control the constitutional result. The project applies controlling federal doctrine to the best documented public facts, then records state-law overlays separately where they provide additional protection or a materially different analysis.

    The project distinguishes three different forms of authority:

    1. Judicial determination — a court has classified the actual space or a materially identical forum.
    2. Agency position — the government has formally described the space, its purpose, or the expressive uses it permits or restricts.
    3. Field Guide determination — the project's legal assessment from controlling/analogous authority plus documented site facts.

    An agency position is evidence. It is not a substitute for constitutional analysis.

    Required zone record¶

    Every meaningful public-access zone should carry:

    • stable facility ID and zone ID;
    • zone name and physical description;
    • public-access status;
    • federal forum classification: TPF, DPF, LPF, or NPF;
    • federal legal confidence;
    • state-law forum classification where materially distinct;
    • geometric/boundary confidence;
    • factual basis for classification;
    • judicial authorities and whether each is exact or analogous;
    • agency position, if one is located;
    • ownership/control/ROW/easement evidence;
    • restriction audits by activity;
    • current signs/rules observed or sourced;
    • unresolved factual question, if any;
    • a concrete research task for every unresolved factual question.

    Two confidences, not one¶

    Legal confidence measures how strongly doctrine and established facts support the forum classification.

    Boundary confidence measures how precisely the project can place that classified zone on the ground.

    These must not be collapsed. A municipal sidewalk may be a high-confidence TPF even while the exact transition between sidewalk and an adjoining forecourt remains medium-confidence geometry.

    No unexplained UNRESOLVED¶

    UNRESOLVED is permitted only when a specific missing fact could materially change the classification. Every unresolved zone must state:

    • the missing fact;
    • why it matters;
    • the public record, field observation, or other source expected to resolve it;
    • the associated research task.

    "No court has ruled on this exact square foot" is not by itself a valid unresolved reason. The project applies existing doctrine to the documented facts.

    Map geometry¶

    Forum-map geometry should progress through three levels:

    1. Schematic — top-down research diagram sufficient to visualize zones, explicitly not a survey.
    2. Evidence-aligned — geometry traced from official site plans, parcel/ROW records, easements, or comparable authoritative mapping.
    3. Field-hardened — evidence-aligned geometry checked against current physical conditions/signage where boundary-dependent enforcement is plausible.

    The UI must always disclose the current geometry level and must not imply survey precision that the evidence does not support.

    Judicial-source sweep¶

    Before finalizing a facility map, research must search for decisions involving:

    • the exact facility;
    • the agency/library system;
    • the same facility type in the controlling federal circuit;
    • materially analogous physical spaces under Supreme Court precedent;
    • state constitutional cases where the state-law overlay is relevant.

    Exact-site holdings outrank analogies. Analogies remain usable when their factual similarities and differences are documented.

    Member research workflow¶

    A forum map is a research surface, not merely a finished illustration. Members should be able to select a zone and see:

    • current Field Guide determination;
    • confidence and evidence basis;
    • authorities;
    • restriction posture;
    • agency position;
    • missing evidence;
    • open map-hardening tasks.

    Submitted records follow the ordinary immutable research/review workflow. Approval of a source does not automatically alter a forum classification; an Administrator-approved classification change should cite the approved evidence and preserve the prior determination in history.

    Walnut Creek pilot¶

    CA-013-F0002 is the first map pilot because its Registry record already has area-specific federal determinations:

    • North Broadway / Lincoln ordinary municipal sidewalks — TPF / high;
    • Broadway-Lincoln civic plaza / formal exterior approach — TPF / high;
    • Civic Park entrance/connective route and Civic Park — TPF / high;
    • library surface lot — TPF / high;
    • ordinary library interior — LPF / medium;
    • Oak View Room — LPF / high;
    • Las Trampas Conference Room — LPF / high;
    • group study rooms — LPF / medium;
    • underground public garage — NPF / medium;
    • garage circulation — NPF / medium;
    • cafe — unresolved pending control/access facts.

    The initial member map is schematic. The first geometry-hardening tasks are to locate the controlling parcel/ROW/easement evidence and an official final/as-built site circulation plan, then align the SVG zones to those records.
